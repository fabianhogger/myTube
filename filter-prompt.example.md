I want my feed to show me videos that teach me something durable or that I would
genuinely choose to spend half an hour on. Judge each video on its title,
channel, description, duration and age.

**Pass** a video when it looks like:
- A technical deep-dive, talk, lecture, or detailed explainer.
- Long-form documentary, investigative journalism, or original reporting.
- A craft or build video where someone makes something real.
- Music or a live performance from a channel I subscribe to.

**Block** a video when it looks like:
- Reaction content, drama, gossip, or commentary on another creator.
- Clickbait framing: ALL CAPS, "you won't believe", manufactured shock, numbered
  listicles with no substance, or a thumbnail-era title with no actual claim.
- Shorts, or anything under about 3 minutes that isn't music.
- Engagement-farming news hot takes and algorithm-chasing trend-hopping.
- Sponsored or affiliate content where the product *is* the video.

When a video is genuinely ambiguous, block it — I would rather have a short feed
I trust than a long one I have to re-filter in my head.
